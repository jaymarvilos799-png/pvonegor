Feedback and complaints

Tell us how we did

How to send feedback or file a complaint, and how the office acts on them.

How to send feedback

Answer the Customer Satisfaction Feedback Form at the office and put it in the feedback and complaints drop box.

How feedback is processed

Assigned personnel receive and verify the nature of queries and feedback within one (1) working day, then refer them to the concerned office by phone or email. Feedback is reviewed for its content, relevance and possible impact. Depending on its nature, action may be taken to address issues, make improvements or implement changes.

How to file a complaint

Email the following details to pvonegor@gmail.com, or submit them to the Administrative Officer V and/or the Provincial Veterinarian:

  • Your full name and contact information
  • A narrative of the complaint
  • Evidence
  • Name of the person being complained about

Include a clear description of the complaint.

How complaints are processed

The responsible division or unit acknowledges receipt of every complaint and determines whether it is technical or non-technical. Non-technical complaints are investigated with the parties involved and resolved, which may include corrective or disciplinary action; if the resolution is not satisfactory, the complaint may be forwarded to the Provincial Grievance Committee. Technical complaints are addressed by the chief of the concerned division or unit, who sends the client a letter on the action taken, noted by the Provincial Veterinarian. The effectiveness of the action is reported to top management of the Provincial Capitol.

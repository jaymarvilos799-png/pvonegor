Organization

The sections, staff and responsibilities of the Office of the Provincial Veterinarian.

How the office is organized

Six sections, one mandate

Per the office's Citizen's Charter (2026, 3rd Edition), the Provincial Veterinary Office carries out its work through six sections built around three core functions: Production, Protection, and Regulation.

Handles disease prevention, surveillance and clinical care for livestock, poultry and pets, and runs the Provincial Animal Disease Diagnostic Laboratory (PADDL).

Sample services

  • Consultation, vaccination, deworming & treatment
  • Rabies examination (dFAT)
  • Blood sample collection for poultry, swine, goat, cattle & carabao
  • cPCR testing for African Swine Fever (ASF)
  • Fecal examination (fecalysis)

Good to know

  • Pets must be at least 3 months old for rabies vaccination, 2 weeks for deworming
  • ASF cPCR testing: free
  • Rabies examination: ₱250 per sample
  • Fecal exam: ₱10 per sample
  • Most walk-in consultations are completed same day

Regulates the movement of animals and animal products in and out of the province, and processes the certificates that keep the trade in livestock, poultry and meat safe and traceable.

Sample services

  • Certificate of Acceptance & Downtime Compliance Certificate
  • Certificate of Free Status for Avian Influenza (CFS-AI)
  • Accreditation of locally registered meat vans
  • Upgrading of ASF zoning status for LGUs
  • Ocular inspection of animal facilities

Good to know

  • Meat van accreditation: ₱300 · Acceptance/Downtime Certificate: ₱50
  • Inbound shipments follow EO No. 54 s.2022 and EO No. 10 s.2024
  • ASF zone progression is a highly technical, multi-step process for LGUs

Advances the province's livestock genetics and supplies breeding stock, semen and liquid nitrogen to farmers, LGUs and cooperatives.

Sample services

  • Artificial insemination for cattle, carabao, goat & swine
  • Semen & liquid nitrogen (LN2) availment
  • Purchase of stock — pullets, gilts, boars, bucks
  • Stock dispersal for chicks, pullets & goats
  • Technical advice on livestock & poultry production

Good to know

  • Semen straw: ₱50 · Day-old chick: ₱30 · Pullet: ₱75 · Piglet: ₱1,800
  • Stock dispersal requires an approved Letter of Intent from the Governor
  • Most artificial insemination visits are completed within 6 hours

Supports safe food handling from farm to market — inspecting local livestock auction markets, abattoirs, meat establishments and storage facilities across the province.

Sample services

  • Technical assistance to local livestock "Oksyon" markets (LOMs)
  • Abattoir, meat establishment & storage facility inspection
  • Assessment and recommendations to LGUs on food-safety compliance

Good to know

  • Requests are submitted by LGUs and typically resolved within 3 days
  • Inspections are conducted jointly with LGU personnel

Brings the office's services out into the field, and connects farmers, students and partner organizations with hands-on training and technical support.

Sample services

  • Animal welfare missions, AI & heat synchronization outreach
  • Speakerships in seminars, trainings & workshops
  • On-the-job training placement for students
  • Research & thesis data assistance

Good to know

  • Outreach requests come from LGUs, agencies, and farmer associations or cooperatives
  • Bring a letter of request naming the date, venue and services needed

Keeps the office running — from client transactions to payroll, procurement and the records that support every field mission.

Sample services

  • Disbursement vouchers, purchase & job orders
  • Certificate of employment, payslip & service record
  • Travel orders & leave applications
  • Feedback and complaints handling

Good to know

  • Feedback forms are available on-site and reviewed within 1 working day
  • Complaints can be filed by email with a narrative and evidence

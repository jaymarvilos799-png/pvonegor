Who we serve

One office, every municipality

Negros Oriental's 25 cities and municipalities each rely on the Provincial Veterinary Office for services their local units can't provide on their own — from a single backyard hog raiser to a barangay-wide vaccination drive.

Farmers & raisersLivestock and poultry health, breeding, shipping permits
Pet ownersVaccination, deworming, spay/neuter, consultations
LGUs & barangaysVaccination drives, disease surveillance, IEC materials
BusinessesMeat inspection, slaughterhouse regulation, farm accreditation

Mandate, mission & vision

Why the Provincial Veterinary Office exists and the values that guide its work.

Mandate

Established under the Local Government Code

Under Article XIX, Section 489 of Republic Act 7160 — the Local Government Code of 1991 — the Provincial Veterinarian is tasked with the delivery of veterinary services, the development of the livestock and poultry industry, and the enforcement of laws protecting animal welfare and public health.

"Be in the frontline of veterinary-related activities, particularly in the outbreak of highly contagious and deadly animal diseases and in situations resulting in the depletion of animals for work or human consumption."
Formulate measuresFor the Sangguniang Panlalawigan & technical support to the Governor
Plan & implementStrategies for animal production, including artificial insemination
Regulate & inspectAnimal movement, abattoirs, auction markets, meat & dairy products
Disease controlPrevent, control, eradicate & treat animal diseases
AdviseThe Governor & Sanggunian on livestock and poultry policy

Mission

To deliver effective veterinary services that sustain and improve the province's animal industry, while protecting the public from the risk of animal-borne disease.

Vision

A dynamic regulatory and development agency, providing responsive veterinary and husbandry services for the welfare and growth of the province's livestock and poultry sectors.

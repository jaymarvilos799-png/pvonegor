Executive orders & resolutions

Provincial issuances related to African Swine Fever, newest first.

PPCC Resolution No. 1 s.2026 Suggested Retail Price of Swine

Negros Oriental Provincial Price Coordinating Council, approved 6 August 2026. Suggested prices during the ASF outbreak: pork (local and imported) ₱240–₱290 per kg, piglets ₱2,500–₱5,000 per head, and live hogs (buying price, live weight) ₱150–₱180 per kg

Download

EO No. 46 S.2026_Complete Ban on the Entry of Live Hogs and Their Products and By-products into the province

Signed by Governor Manuel L. Sagarbarria on 24 June 2026. Bans the entry of live pigs, pork, pork products and by-products, frozen boar semen and hog transport vehicles from ASF-affected, restricted or high-risk areas, and reactivates the Provincial ASF Task Force

PDF · 4 pages, long bond · 947 KB

Download

Guidelines on the Movement of Swine and its Products and By-products into the Province

Executive Order No. 10, s. 2024, issued by Governor Manuel L. Sagarbarria on 21 February 2024. Sets the ASF zones, the documents required to bring live pigs, genetic materials, and frozen, processed and canned pork products into the province, and the 7-day downtime and disinfection rules for livestock transport vehicles

Animal welfare laws & issuances — quick-reference summary +

The office's regulatory and welfare work is grounded in national law and Department of Agriculture issuances. This is a quick-reference summary, not the full text.

Core legislation

  • RA 8485 — Animal Welfare Act of 1998
  • RA 10631 — amends certain sections of RA 8485 (2013)
  • RA 9482 — Anti-Rabies Act of 2007

Facility registration & standards

  • Registration rules for pet shops, kennels, pounds, shelters, farms, zoos & slaughterhouses
  • Code of Standards for Veterinary Clinics & Hospitals
  • Rules on dog shows & import/export registry for dogs and cats

Species-specific welfare codes

  • Code of Practice for the Welfare of Chickens
  • Code of Practice for the Welfare of Pigs
  • Welfare of horses in breeding farms, stables & race tracks

Transport & humane handling

  • Transport of live animals by land, sea & air
  • Humane handling in the slaughter of animals for food
  • Revised rules on euthanasia of animals

Enforcement & observance

  • Deputation & training of Animal Welfare Enforcement Officers (AWEOs)
  • Rules on apprehending & prosecuting illegal dog/cat transport and trade
  • Animal Welfare Week — first week of October, annually

Compiled from the DA-Bureau of Animal Industry's Compendium of Animal Welfare Legislation, Policies and Issuances in the Philippines (2021). The full compendium runs to 300 pages and roughly 19 MB, too large to host on this page — request a copy from the office or the Bureau of Animal Industry.
